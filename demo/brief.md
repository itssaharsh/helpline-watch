# Demo brief

1. One-liner: Helpline Watch turns "which brand" into a takedown pack of the fake customer-care numbers scammers planted on Google, city by city.
2. Audience: four SerpApi developer advocates judging a 3-minute local-run recording once, sound on.
3. The aha: the console at /app: the globe pulses through five cities, the findings fill, and a number in the Places box on the Mumbai page gets its FAKE mark; then Evidence shows seven named reasons and Across brands shows the same number touching three brands.
4. Problem hook: 1.73 lakh complaints and over ₹2,100 crore lost in India to fake customer-care numbers found on Google; the analyst today googles from one desk and cannot see what a victim in Patna sees.
5. Scope cut: no city picker, no brand switching, no mark-as-official, no log tab, no kit page; one brand (HDFC Bank), one sweep, one pack.
